Target gains a [[Diseased]] Marker.  
Select another Enemy model within 3" of the Target and repeat this Ki Feat, at no cost, on the new model; ignoring Range, LoS and Camouflage.  
Continue until an Opposed Ki roll is failed.  
No model may be affected more than once by this Ki Feat in a Turn.  
If the Enemy model is BtB with a Friendly Rat model, it may not benefit from Spirit or Resistance for this Opposed Ki Test.  
The Target is always considered Surprised by this Ki Feat.
