In a Melee Exchange, the Enemy model (and its Melee Weapons) lose [[Kata]], [[Feint]], [[Brutal]], [[Parry]], [[Dodge]], [[Unblockable]] and [[Impenetrable Defence]].  
This model gains any Traits the Enemy model loses with the same values.  
This Ki Feat ends at the end of the current Activation.
