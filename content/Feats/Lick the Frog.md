When a model that is not Immune [Poison] and is not a Monk or Sohei model, is in BtB with this model, performs a Focus Action it gains double its Ki Statistic in Ki instead of just its Ki Statistic.  
That model also gains a [[Poisoned]] (X) Marker where X is equal to the Ki gained by the action.
