You may use this Ki Feat when a Friendly unarmoured Courtier Model within 4" is selected or targeted by an Enemy model's declared Melee, Charge or Range Attack Action.  
This model swaps positions with the Target Courtier model.  
Continue with the Declared Action with this model, including any Damage rolls.
