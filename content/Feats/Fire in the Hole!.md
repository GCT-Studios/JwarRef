Remove one Ammo Counter from this model.  
Select a point on the Battlefield within 5" and place a Grenade Terrain element.  
In the next Starting Phase, centre a 2" Pulse on that terrain.  
Models in the Pulse gain a Fire (2) Marker and Stunned Marker, then remove this terrain from the Battlefield.
