When this model Charges, it may move through models and ignore ZoC.  
Each model that you own moved through may be placed in BtB with the Charge Target if possible.  
Models Placed in this way may remove any Controlled, Diseased, On Fire, Poisoned, Prone, or Spirit Block Markers.
