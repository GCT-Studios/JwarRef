For the next Damage roll, this model's Recurve Bow weapon gains +X Weapon Strength where X is the number of marked Wound Boxes on the Target's card.  
After the Damage roll, you may have the Target lose Y Ki Tokens or gain Y Bleed (1) Markers where Y is equal to the final Damage (ignoring Durable if a Kami model) that would have been inflicted. If you do, this Ranged Attack causes no Damage.
