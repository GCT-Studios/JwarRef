**Q: Can a Giant Eagle or Eagle of the Jwar Isles use their Unique Effects to go into [[Flank]] and then [[Deploy]] during the same turn?**  
A: No, see the updated Flank trait.  

**Q: Do Control Markers affect the Variable Statistics on the Blood Brothers?**  
A: No.  Their Unique Effect states that it references the warband that recruited them originally and only checks that they have not been removed from play. The variable statistics are not changed when one or more of them are in the controlled state.  

**Q: Does Lord Toad placing a VIM elsewhere remove the VIM status?**  
A: Yes  

**Q: For the Equipment Card The Kiyozumi Katana, does a “Kiyozumi family model” refer to a model with Kiyozumi name or a model recruited into the Kiyozumi Family theme?**  
A: A model recruited into the Theme.
